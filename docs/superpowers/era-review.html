2026-08-09 — the harness audited itself, and the answer was not the one being chased

42 agents · 17 commits

Capture determinism is SOLVED, and it was never the renderer. menu.png is now 0.0000% run-to-run — byte-identical across two separate processes, from 0.984 / 0.455 / 0.359 / 0.557%. The cause was if (isCaptureMode()) return;, which freezes an animation wherever it got to; capture is enabled after a boot whose length varies 1.68–10.43 s, so the frozen phase was run-dependent. Pairwise frame diff tracked that window at r = 0.842. Byte-identical frames went 13/31 → 23/30.

But the framing was wrong. Every defect this harness has actually shipped was coverage or content, never tolerance. menu needs 61,440 changed pixels to fail the 6% gate and produced ~10,000 at worst — it could not go red — while ocean-coast sat genuinely red at 8.34% and 13 of 31 frames reproduce byte-identically behind a threshold ~1000× looser than they warrant. The live defect was false NEGATIVES on the static frames.

The largest hole had never been examined by anyone: the baselines ARE the specification, and no gate touched them. 79 of 1,603 commits rewrite them, and 10 of the last 12 bundled the src change that moved the pixels — which makes an intended look change indistinguishable from a regression the oracle was quietly updated to match. That is how four beast-* baselines stayed pictures of an empty mountain for weeks. There is now a trailer gate that demands a justification and forbids the bundling, plus an old/new/diff contact sheet; today's re-baseline is the first commit in this repo's history to pass through it.

Two recorded conclusions were killed by measurement, not argument. The recorded next step — --deterministic-mode and --font-render-hinting — turned out to be absent from the Chrome binary puppeteer actually launches, verified with strings over the real framework and a presence control so the instrument had to prove it could say yes. And the device-pixel-ratio pin, recorded as a confirmed 2.7× win, was arithmetically inert. A switch in a source tree is not a switch in a binary; grep the artifact you run.

Also closed: CI now LOADS the production bundle Vercel deploys (100% of testing hit a build that never ships, 0% the one that does); GL shader-link failures and lost contexts are fatal rather than a printed warning; the gate asserts its own frame denominator; the intra-page invariant is standing; and .claude/settings.json gives the repo its first agent-boundary enforcement — the CLI whose fall-through target is the caller's own session is denied, not merely warned about.

E12 · the grass, and the instrument that was measuring it

2026-08-08 · 13 commits

The feature was the smaller half. S8 gave every blade its own yaw, scale, sub-cell offset and tint, hashed from the world (x,z) so the capture gate still byte-compares — the whole field had been one rectangle repeated on a 2m lattice, swaying in lockstep because the shader phase was x*0.5 + z*0.5, constant along every diagonal. S9 then put it in the lighting equation and dropped transparency, which halves grass draw calls: three splits transparent + DoubleSide into two passes at WebGLRenderer.js:922 and :1619, so every grass draw-call figure in these docs was 2× low.

Then S8 broke capture determinism, and the correction outranks the feature. explore-day's run-to-run self-diff went 0.075% → 1.646%, and the pixels that moved were the distant treeline, not the grass. The first reading — "pre-existing chunk streaming" — was stated with confidence and was wrong; a ten-minute pre-S8 control reversed it. Root cause: capture.mjs polled the chunk count and then slept a fixed delay(2500) for a mesh swap-in it admitted the count could not see. A sleep encodes an assumption about frame cost that any commit can invalidate. It is now a condition (waitForStableFrame), and back to 0.083%. B-race had been closed thirty-seven minutes before S8 landed — the same morning, not days earlier — as "the harness is trustworthy again", on three agreeing runs. Determinism is a claim about a code state; it expires when the code does. Three agreeing runs is not determinism for the same reason one is not — and all of it sat under the 6% gate, which reported green throughout.

Two gates that were green over nothing. The 31 gate frames come from just six camera poses (capture.mjs, y 26–82), and the explore pose that frames the grass sits ~18m above the spawn surface, where a 0.7m tuft is a few pixels: S8 measured 3.119% against baseline and S9 1.034%, with 0 of 31 over the 6% threshold in both cases. The visual gate would have passed this work whether or not it functioned. And the first colour ladder built to settle the palette separated its own three options by 294 pixels — 0.027% — at night; a decision aid that cannot distinguish its own options is not one.

The plan's own ordering rule was necessary but not sufficient. S9's spec predicts that lighting the grass before S8 makes it darker (one shared yaw → N·L ≤ 0) and prescribes ordering as the cure. S8 shipped first — and the grass came out darker anyway, at a measured 4:1 skew of tuft pixels below the ground's median luminance, reading as holes punched in the turf. The mechanism was never the yaw: with DoubleSide three rebuilds the normal per fragment from gl_FrontFacing, so the lit face follows the rasterizer — and the sun sits beyond the field, in front of the camera, so the shot is back-lit and the camera-facing side is the unlit one. Rotating a card cannot fix a normal that follows the viewer. Bending the shading normal toward world-up — the standard foliage-card treatment — took it to 2.6:1. Two tidier explanations for the remainder were measured dead rather than argued: more world-up is worse, and matching the terrain's own MeshStandardMaterial is identical.

Decided, not escalated. The colour was held back one iteration precisely because a colour chosen under wrong lighting gets chosen twice — then Kevin took it off his own desk: "decide the grass colour too… only leave really important / non-reversible / critical ones for me." Swatch B #5E8A3E shipped at 83054c1: +8/+14/+9 on the ground's own #567C35, replacing a blue-green that read as a different plant from the turf. The measurement ruled out A outright and could not separate B from C, so that half was decided on stated reasoning rather than a number invented to look objective.

The five rules

every scar, encoded
01 · MUTATION-PROVE

Break the behaviour → it must go RED → revert via cp-backup, never git checkout. A gate never seen red is a rubber stamp.

02 · VERIFY THEN ASSERT

Every external/absence/root-cause claim gets a live check the same turn. The registry is a hypothesis.

03 · GREEN ≠ LIVED

A green headless gate isn't a lived result. Audio / render / feel → a lived probe or Kevin. Pure logic → a pure unit.

04 · NEVER WEAKEN TO PASS

A gate green on day one against unfixed code proves nothing. Assume vacuous until seen red.

05 · NOT SELF-JUDGED

The worker may not certify its own completion. Findings are adversarially verified before they count.
