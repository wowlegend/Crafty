2026-10-29, new: W2-D, the HUD (90 to 99)

  1. 90

    The desktop rail goes, the level card folds into the hotbar and quests rest on one line; nothing covers a 48 px box at the screen’s centre, and each touch screen gets one of three layouts by its shape (tall, phone, phone on its side). Why: about a fifth of a 1280x800 frame was interface.

  2. 91

    The compass is a component whose labels yield in priority order (boss, home, shrine, Blight Heart, villager, chest, cardinal), and its capture guards are not carried over; three source-grep gates are replaced by one that drives the real marker builder.

  3. 92

    One notification channel: a key renders through t() when shown, the same key inside 4 s shows once, and a plain denial is never deduped, since each answers a press. On a phone the line holds one notice: the dragon’s one-off call, else the newest plain notice, else its attack call. Why last: the attack calls repeat all fight, and ranked first they left a notice raised mid-fight at most 0.8 s of its 4 s; across 11,520 simulated fights a notice now holds its whole life.

  4. 93–94

    The 250 ms mob mirror is deleted; the minimap, compass and lookbook read the live queries. The chrome share is a footprint: the pixels the HUD’s own elements change on a held page, palette-free, because counting surface colours read warm stone as chrome (7.46% of a world with none). Its blind spot, warm chrome over a warm world, is pinned by a fixture.

  5. 95–96
