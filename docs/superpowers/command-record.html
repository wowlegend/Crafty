The Cut, step 4: talents and relics (PR #44)

dadbee65on main 09:04Z · new
  • A level pays for itself. Each level adds 2 strength, 1 agility and 2 intellect on its own, so damage, health and mana rise with it, and pays one talent point. The attribute panel and Spell Mastery are gone; a spell casts at the tier its level reached, III at level 5 (e2fc90ad, items 41 and 42). A level’s damage sits 1 to 8% above what a player who spread points evenly dealt.
  • Every talent changes how a verb plays. Each Aspect tree’s root unlocks its verb; then Primal Endurance lengthens the roar 3 s a rank, Twin Grasp lets a grab hold two blocks, Wide Snare doubles the snare’s aim cone, and Chain Imbue makes the zone leap to one more hostile within 8 m, among others. Each tree ends in two capstones, and taking one shuts the other; respec refunds everything (acfcd022, item 44).
  • Relics drop from hostiles, each at 4 to 6%, into one Relic slot, and work the same way: Ember Fang (+4 s roar), Lodestone (+1 grab throw), Snare Knot (a wider snare), Prism Shard (+1 leap). Hovering one says what it does (item 45).
  • Gone: crystals, the wand and its mana discount (a level-1 Fireball costs 15 mana, not 14), and affixes; the merchant sells potions for coins (e9f21830, item 46). Old saves: a save from before the Cut gets a talent point per five attribute points, and every talent rank back, once, with a notice; an e2e reads the refund from a real saved blob (997d0a97, item 43).
  • What a stat build loses. Before the Cut, 20 of the 26 talents added a stat. A level-10 player with an even build and nine points in intellect talents cast a non-crit Fireball III for 264; that level casts it for 187 now (-29%), and the four armour talents have no successor. A row asks whether such a build is owed anything.
  • How it got here. Two review rounds changed nine rules (items 48 and 49). A grab’s kept block waited out the first throw’s 1.5 s flight, which also blocked a slam and ate the click, so the hurl now flies up to six blocks at once; a relic swapped mid-verb no longer keeps its effect; a chained imbue no longer stacks two zones of one element on a body for twice the damage; and every save records the refund as paid, so a rollback cannot pay it twice. The third round left one LOW row, for corrupt saves.
  • Not proven: the feel of the trees and the relic drop rates (no playtest); the grab’s second throw, the wider snare, the chained imbue and the relics in the running game (unit-tested; the e2e drives the roar); the Talents page fitting one screen; WebKit and touch.

W2-E, the hands and the eye (PR #43)

3e138271on main 08:15Z · new
  • Two warm ember gauntlets replace the two near-black slabs and the orange sphere: an ember-gold plate over a leather bracer, a white-gold cuff and a glowing ember core; the wand and the held block sit in the fists at hand size (8eca5ff2, item 33). They draw in their own pass, nearer than any wall, with a fixed 60 degree view and no shadow, so they keep their size at speed and never sink into a wall: at a tree trunk they keep 0.97 of the hand pixels they show 3.5 m back, where the old hands kept 0.02.
  • Readable at midnight. The hand pixels’ mean brightness went from 28.7 to 100.2 and the median from 7 to 106, against a floor of 48 and 40 declared before shooting. The key light on the hands follows the world’s shadow, so in a cave or under a roof they no longer take full sun, and the storm’s flash reaches them (item 38). On an iPad in portrait both fists are now in frame.
  • The eye sits at 1.62 m, not 2.15 m (8a7c748a, item 34). The old eye stood 0.30 m above the top of the head: blocks read small, you looked down on every mob, and in a two-block passage the eye sat inside the ceiling block. Melee reach is unchanged. Mobs measure you from the eye, so one step up is no longer a refuge from ordinary melee, while a two-block pillar still is (item 36).
  • Turning the view no longer re-renders the sun’s shadow map; the old hands were the only reason a camera move counted as a shadow caster (81ff88ac).
  • How it got here. The first reviews found the roar’s reveal camera still opening 0.53 m above the new eye (986243cf) and the mob reach the lower eye moved (item 36). A /code-review xhigh then found 15 issues, among them the swing and the heavy charge sweeping the fist off the frame at the new 60 degree view (item 37). The last re-review found the arcane golem’s translucent halo still around the eye in beast form: item 39 now hides only the beast’s boxes, and that row is open again. W2-K’s dark polygon between the hands was a mesh of the old hands, deleted with them (item 35).
  • Open: the beast form’s halo around the eye (MED); the gauntlets’ swing, cast and charge poses never seen on a GPU (MED); mobs sensing you from the shaking camera (MED); a per-frame allocation in the hand pose and the beast hide reading the previous frame’s camera (LOW); the touch controls over the gauntlets on an iPad in portrait; the second pass’s GPU cost. Not checked on the deployed site or a real iPad.
