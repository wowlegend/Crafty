# Adversarial refute of the roadmap (adversarial-reviewer-kz, xhigh, 2026-10-27)

**Verdict: KEEP the shape of the roadmap, but 10 of its 14 moves need RESIZE or REORDER before anyone builds them.** Two findings are design defects backed by arithmetic on the code: move 5's speeds fail its own proof, and move 2's idle-shadow target cannot be met while the sun moves. Move 11 also misses a heat source that no lane found: point lights mounting and unmounting on treasure chests.

## Findings, most severe first

**F1. CONFIRMED (HIGH). Move 5's speeds fail its own proof, and its premise that you "cannot outwalk a fight" stays false.**
- What the evidence measures is mob chase speed against the proposed walk speeds, which is exactly what the claim needs.
- Chase speed is base speed × 1.5 when aggro'd, × 2.0 for spiders (`ai.worker.js:327` `const speedMult = (isAggro ? (type === 'spider' ? 2.0 : 1.5) : 1.0)`).
- Base speeds (`mobTypes.js:9-20`): Husk (`zombie`) 2.0 → 3.0 m/s; skeleton → 3.75; emberhusk → 3.45; spider → 6.0; brute 1.2 → 1.8.
- The proposed backpedal is 6.5 × 0.8 = 5.2 m/s, and strafing is 6.5.
- Failure: the move's own e2e ("held S against an aggro'd husk, gap closes within 3 s") stays RED by construction, because the gap grows by 2.2 m/s. With hound and skitterling raised to 6, a backpedal still outruns 4 of 7 hostiles, and a strafe outruns all of them.
- Meanwhile the walk falls from Kevin's 14 m/s (`locomotion.js:3`, "travel felt slow, esp. to the far Blight-Heart — Kevin"). Under move 10's siege, night play is nearly always "in combat", so it stays slow.
- The "+4° FOV kick" is redundant: `Components.jsx:1283` `targetFov = 75 + (horizontalSpeed - 5) * 1.5` already gives about 88.5° at 14 m/s.
- Fix: re-derive the speeds from the roster table, so backpedal is below the slowest intended chaser.

**F2. CONFIRMED (HIGH, heat budget). Move 2(c)'s shadow-on-demand cannot reach "0 shadow renders standing still".**
- A full day/night cycle is 300 s (`dayNight.js:20-26`, "4 units/s => ... a 5-minute full cycle").
- The sun's azimuth swings 90° each way along its arc (`mood.js:146,161` `a = aNoon + SUN_ARC_SWING * Math.cos(theta)`), which is roughly 1.6–1.9°/s.
- At that rate the proposed ">0.05°" trigger fires about every 26–31 ms, which is 30+ Hz, standing still or not.
- So the idle-zero target and much of the "40% below live" saving are unattainable as written.
- Fix: quantise the sun into steps (for example 0.5°, about every 0.3 s), and state the remaining update rate.
- needs repro: script R2 below.

**F3. CONFIRMED (MED-HIGH). Move 11's 4-light pool misses most of the code paths that change the light count, and its "net negative" heat claim ignores the pool's own cost.** No lane surfaced this.
- Point lights that mount and unmount during play:
  - Every treasure chest carries a beacon light, `Terrain.jsx:424` `<pointLight position={[0, 1.1, 0]} intensity={1.5} distance={6} color="#ffd700" />`.
  - Chests spawn every 30 s, up to 5 unopened (`QuestSystem.jsx:725,752`), plus uncapped shrine chests (`:823`), and are removed when opened (`:897`).
  - The cast hand mounts a light per cast (`playerRender.jsx:506-512` `{attackType === 'spell' && ... <pointLight`).
  - The beast form mounts one (`BeastAvatar.jsx:164`), and so does the boss (`BossEntity.jsx:565,590`).
- Failure (a): the light count changes every chest spawn or open and every cast, so the "renderer.info.programs constant across 20 casts" proof can go red from `playerRender.jsx:512` even after the pool lands.
- Failure (b): at steady state about 5 chest lights and the brazier sit in every lit fragment for the whole session. Adding 4 always-mounted pool lights is a permanent per-fragment cost at about 7.7 Mpx. The upside's L5 inference states the mechanism: lights at intensity 0 still cost.
- Fix: put the chest beacons and the cast hand light into the pool too, and size the pool per tier.

**F4. CONFIRMED (MED). Phase 1 bundles the 147→151 re-baseline with move 4's re-baseline.**
- Roadmap: "Move 4 lands together with the owed 147-to-151 re-baseline as one Baseline-Review commit".
- The upside's own door (d) says "never bundle the renderer rebaseline ... and the look pass in one rewrite, or no change can be attributed".
- OVERNIGHT.md:124-126 keeps a same-renderer A/B per change for exactly this reason.
- Failure: the 151 oracle never exists on its own, so later 151 drift cannot be told apart from palette drift.
- Fix: re-baseline 147→151 alone first.

**F5. CONFIRMED mechanism, PLAUSIBLE outcome (MED). Move 4 ignores the capture solar pin and the sun-direction seam.**
- Capture pins the sun at noon for every frame: `Atmosphere.jsx:130` `CAPTURE_SOLAR_FRACTION = 0.25` and `:246` `const cycle = cap ? CAPTURE_SOLAR_FRACTION : ...`.
- Failure: an elevation-keyed script renders the explore-night, dusk and boss capture frames as noon, so the oracle stops depicting night. The fix is a declared fraction per capture state; the upside saw this and the roadmap did not.
- The move-4 evidence image is affected too. I opened `explore-night.png`: it is a frame with the night palette and a noon sun, a state that never occurs in play. The palette claim stands only on `mood.js:99-102`.
- Seam: `Atmosphere.jsx:279-280` writes `sunDirRef` from the sun direction, and the parked `oreGlow.js:66` `oreNight = 1.0 - smoothstep(..., uSunDir.y)` reads it. If the moon handoff writes that direction, ores never glow at night and cloud shadows follow the moon. The move does not say to keep a separate key-light vector.

**F6. CONFIRMED (MED). Move 4's bloom threshold above 1.0 breaks the parked ore tuning.**
- `wt/world/oreGlow.js:14-15` reads "Diamond reaches the bloom threshold" with `{ iron: 0.35, gold: 0.8, diamond: 1.25 }` multiplied by linear texture colour. That was tuned against `GameScene.jsx:121` `luminanceThreshold={0.65}`.
- With the threshold above 1, none of the three ores blooms. That contradicts treating the parked work as "in hand".
- Fix: re-tune and re-prove the ores in the same unit.

**F7. CONFIRMED (MED). Move 1's oscillation evidence and several of its proofs do not measure what they claim.**
- "Red on today's code, where the probe records the oscillation": the probe reads the tier at only three moments, `ipad-probe.mjs:104-107` (`+3s`, `+10s`, `+20s`).
- The probe's own blind spot (`:17-22`): "Frame rate is load-dependent on this machine ... a clean run here does not clear the iPad". So tier changes per minute in that harness measure the Mac host, not the iPad. T7 is still open (`ACTIVE_PLAN.md:29`), so no run is on record.
- Mechanism (`PerformanceMonitor.js:7-9,58-69`): a decision every 2.5 s, and a decline needs 8 of 10 samples below 40 fps. The ~5 s loop happens only if med or high sustains below 40 fps; PLAUSIBLE, unmeasured.
- The "advance() under 55% of the interval" headroom rule times the CPU side. FrameCap's own header (`:4-5`) says the problem is "a GPU pinned at 100% while holding its frame rate".
- "On the PRODUCTION build" proofs (composer width, effect construction counts, `readFrameloop`) need hooks that only exist in DEV (`FrameCap.jsx:92` `if (!import.meta.env.DEV) return;`).

**F8. CONFIRMED (MED-LOW). Move 1(c) wraps GodRays and N8AO but not Bloom, which is rebuilt the same way.**
- The wrapper memoises on `[JSON.stringify(a)]` (postprocessing `dist/index.js`, the `P=(e,t)=>` wrapper).
- `mipmapBlur={q.bloomMipmap}` differs between low and med: `quality.js:20` false, `:21` true.
- So a low↔med step constructs a new BloomEffect. `BloomSpikeDriver.jsx:23-31` resolves its effect once and keeps driving the dead one; that is the unverified bake-off candidate in `ACTIVE_PLAN.md:104`. The old effect's targets are also not disposed (PLAUSIBLE).
- Composer resize premise, CONFIRMED: `@react-three/postprocessing` 3.0.4 resizes only when `size` changes (`const{gl:d,...,size:x}=V()` … `A(()=>c?.setSize(x.width,x.height),[c,x])`), not when the pixel ratio changes.

**F9. CONFIRMED (MED). Move 6's free roar cannot be triggered on iPad.**

**F10. CONFIRMED (MED-LOW). Move 2(d)'s HUD ticker dies under "Unlimited".**
